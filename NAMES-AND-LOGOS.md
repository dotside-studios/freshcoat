# Names and logos

"Freshcoat", "Freshcoat Studio" and "Davi" are names Dotside Studios uses for
its products. The Freshcoat mark and the Freshcoat Studio logo in
[`brand/`](brand/) are Dotside Studios' artwork, and so is the Davi wordmark,
whose path data is in
[`apps/editor/src/samples/davi-wordmark.ts`](apps/editor/src/samples/davi-wordmark.ts)
and which the Davi card starters draw.

The [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE) covers Freshcoat's code. It grants no
permission to use these names or this artwork beyond describing where the code
came from (section 6 of the license says so), and neither does anything else in
this repository.

## What you may do

- Say that your project is based on Freshcoat, uses Freshcoat, or is a fork of
  Freshcoat, as a plain statement of fact.
- Keep the names and the artwork where they already appear in this code, such
  as the Davi card starters, when you redistribute it unchanged.
- Use the Freshcoat logo to link to or talk about Freshcoat itself.

## What you may not do

- Use "Freshcoat" or "Davi", or a name that could be confused with them, as
  the name of your fork, product or service.
- Ship the Davi wordmark, or a design that uses it, as your own branding. A
  fork that offers card starters should replace the wordmark with its own.
- Ship the Freshcoat logo or mark as your fork's icon or branding. A fork that
  deploys the editor should replace the files in `editor/public/` that come
  from `brand/`.
- Suggest that Dotside Studios made, endorses or supports your fork.

## Questions

If you are unsure whether a use is fine, open an issue and ask before you
ship it.
