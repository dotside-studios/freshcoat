# Freshcoat

Freshcoat produces designs from templates and records. These terms describe
rendering and batch production.

## Language

**Font preparation**:
Resolving the fonts a template requests and making their faces available for
rendering, while preserving fonts explicitly supplied by the host.

**Export plan**:
The ordered output items resolved from a workspace and an export preset,
including each item's record values, side, variant and file name.
